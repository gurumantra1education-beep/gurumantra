hi, we're Guru Mantra 👋

One exam shouldn't decide your career.

Guru Mantra is an MBA and PGDM admission counselling team. We work with students across India, many of whom scored below 60 percentile in CAT, and help them find recognised B-schools that fit their score, budget and goals.

why we exist

Every year, good students are told to "wait a year".

After CAT results, a lot of capable students hear the same advice: your score isn't good enough, drop a year and try again. For many of them, that isn't true. Hundreds of AICTE-approved MBA and PGDM colleges admit through MAT, CMAT, XAT, ATMA and state exams, and weigh academics, work experience and interviews alongside the score.

The hard part is knowing which of those colleges are worth your time and your family's money. College portals list everything and pass your number to call centres. We do the opposite: we sit down with you, look at your profile honestly, and build a shortlist you can actually act on this season.
