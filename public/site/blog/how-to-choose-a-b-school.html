How to choose a B-school: 7 checks before you pay

Approvals, real placement numbers, fees versus salary, and the questions to ask before you accept an MBA or PGDM offer.

By the Guru Mantra team · 31 Oct 2026

An MBA or PGDM is one of the biggest investments you and your family will make. With thousands of colleges to choose from, rankings and brochures alone won't tell you enough. These seven checks will.

1. Approvals and recognition

For a PGDM, the institute should be approved by AICTE. For an MBA, the college should be affiliated to a recognised university. Many good institutes also hold accreditations such as NBA or NAAC. Check approvals on official websites, not only on the brochure.

2. Real placement numbers

Ask for the latest placement report, not a highlight reel. Look at:

  • the average and median package, not just the highest
  • how many students were placed out of the batch
  • which companies actually hired, and for which roles

A single high package can hide a weak batch average.

3. Total cost, not just tuition

Add up tuition, hostel, mess, laptop, study tours and any refundable deposits. The difference between "tuition" and "total cost" can be several lakh rupees.

4. Fees versus salary

A useful rule of thumb: compare the total two-year cost with the average first-year salary. If the course costs ₹12 lakh and the average package is ₹6 lakh, that's a long payback period. If it costs ₹6 lakh with a ₹7 lakh average, the maths is much kinder. Our college finder shows fees and packages side by side.

5. Specialisations you actually want

Check that the college offers your preferred specialisation, such as finance, marketing, HR or business analytics, and that recruiters hire for it there.

6. Location and industry access

Colleges in or near business hubs like Mumbai, Pune, Bangalore and Delhi NCR often find it easier to arrange internships, live projects and guest sessions.

7. Talk to current students

Students in their second year will give you the most honest picture of teaching quality, placements and campus life. Ask the college to connect you, or find them on LinkedIn.

Quick checklist

  • Approval or affiliation verified
  • Latest placement report with average, median and percentage placed
  • Total two-year cost calculated
  • Payback period you're comfortable with
  • Your specialisation offered and hired for
  • Spoken to at least one current student
