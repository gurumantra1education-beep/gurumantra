MAT vs CMAT: which exam should you take?

How MAT and CMAT differ, which colleges accept each, and how to decide if your CAT score didn't go to plan.

By the Guru Mantra team · 31 Oct 2026

If your CAT result wasn't what you hoped for, MAT and CMAT are usually the next two exams students look at. Both are accepted by hundreds of MBA and PGDM colleges, and both give you a second chance in the same admission season. Here's how they compare.

The basics

MAT CMAT
Conducted by AIMA (All India Management Association) NTA (National Testing Agency)
How often Several sessions a year Once a year
Modes Paper-based, computer-based and internet-based options Computer-based
Colleges in our data that accept it 794 624

Exam patterns, fees and dates change, so always check the official MAT and CMAT websites before you register.

When MAT makes more sense

  • You need a score soon. Because MAT runs several times a year, there's often a session you can still take after CAT results.
  • You can sit more than one attempt. If one session goes badly, another is usually a few months away.
  • Your target colleges are private PGDM institutes that list MAT among accepted exams.

When CMAT makes more sense

  • Your target colleges specifically prefer CMAT, including some state and AICTE-approved institutes that use it in their admission process.
  • You want one well-prepared attempt in the same season as CAT and XAT.

Can you take both?

Yes, and many students do. Taking both widens the list of colleges you're eligible for. The syllabus areas overlap a lot with CAT (quantitative aptitude, reasoning, verbal ability), so your CAT preparation carries over.

Our advice

Don't choose an exam in isolation. Shortlist 8–10 colleges you'd genuinely join first, look at the exams each one accepts, then pick the exam that covers most of your list. You can filter colleges by exam on our college finder.
