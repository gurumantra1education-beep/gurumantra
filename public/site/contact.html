Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Ask us on WhatsApp.

Can I get a good MBA below 60 %ile in CAT?+

Yes. Many AICTE-approved MBA and PGDM colleges admit across a wide range of scores and also accept MAT, CMAT, XAT, ATMA and state exams. They weigh academics, work experience and interviews too. We help you pick the ones worth your time and money.

Is counselling actually free?+

Yes. Your one-to-one session and college shortlist are free, with no obligation to apply anywhere.

MBA or PGDM, which one should I pick?+

An MBA is a university degree and is often cheaper at state and university colleges. A PGDM is an AICTE-approved diploma from an autonomous institute, usually with a more industry-updated curriculum. Your budget, city and specialisation decide. Our MBA vs PGDM guide explains more.

Which exams do you cover?+

CAT, XAT, NMAT, SNAP, MAT, CMAT, ATMA, GMAT and state MBA exams including MAH-CET, TANCET, KMAT, Karnataka PGCET, AP ICET, TS ICET and OJEE.

I haven't taken any exam yet. Can you still help?+

Yes. We can help you choose which exams to take based on the colleges and cities you're aiming for, and when to register for each.

Do you help with scholarships and education loans?+

Yes. We point you to colleges with fee waivers and merit scholarships, and guide you on education-loan options.

Do you help with GD, PI and WAT?+

Yes. At lower percentiles the interview often decides more, so we help you prepare for group discussions, personal interviews and written ability tests.

How accurate is the college data on your website?+

Fees, packages and cutoffs come from data published by colleges for the latest cycle. They can change every year, so always confirm with the college before applying. We do this for you during counselling.
