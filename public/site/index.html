MBA or PGDM? Here's the quick version.

Both are respected two-year management programmes. An MBA is a university degree; a PGDM is an AICTE-approved diploma from an autonomous institute. Budget, city and specialisation decide which suits you.

Yes. Many AICTE-approved MBA and PGDM colleges admit across a wide range of scores and also accept MAT, CMAT, XAT, ATMA and state exams. They weigh academics, work experience and interviews too. We help you pick the ones worth your time and money.
