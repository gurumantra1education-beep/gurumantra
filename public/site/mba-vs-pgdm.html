so which one?

Pick based on your situation

Tight budget?

A state or university MBA through MAT, CMAT or your state exam can cost a fraction of a private PGDM.

Want industry exposure?

Good PGDM institutes update their curriculum often and tend to run more live projects and internships.

Fixed on a city?

Start from the city, then compare MBA and PGDM options there. Our college finder filters by state.
