how it works

What's behind your chance

Cutoff for your category

We start with each college's previous-year CAT cutoff. Where the college publishes category-wise cutoffs we use them; IIMs use their official minimums; other government schools get a typical reservation relaxation. Private colleges usually keep one cutoff for all.

Your profile counts at the top

IIMs and other top schools (cutoff 85+) score academics, work experience, gender and academic diversity, not only CAT. Strong or weak profiles move your chance there by up to 2 percentile points. Elsewhere, CAT decides.

Sectional cutoffs

Add your VARC, DILR and QA percentiles. If any section is below a college's published sectional minimum for your category, we flag it, because that alone can stop a call.

quick answers

CAT predictor FAQs

Which colleges can I get with 50–60 percentile in CAT?+

Set the slider to your percentile: you'll see every college whose published cutoff you clear, plus 1,000+ colleges that admit on CAT, MAT or CMAT without a fixed cutoff. Many good private PGDM colleges in Delhi NCR, Pune, Bangalore and Mumbai fall in this range.

How accurate is this predictor?+

It is as good as last year's cutoffs. Final calls depend on the year's applicant pool, sectional scores, profile and GD/PI. Treat Safe and Good chance as likely, Reach as possible, and Dream as a long shot.

I only know my raw score. Can I still use it?+

Yes. Switch to "Score" and enter your marks out of 198. We estimate your percentile from recent CAT score-to-percentile trends. Your scorecard percentile is always more accurate.

Do private colleges have category relaxation?+

Most private PGDM colleges have one cutoff for all categories. IIMs, IITs and government universities relax cutoffs for EWS, NC-OBC, SC, ST and PwD candidates, which the predictor applies when you choose your category.

My percentile is low. Is an MBA still possible?+

Yes. Look at the "Also open to you" list, and consider MAT and CMAT, which have later windows. This is exactly what Guru Mantra helps with: a shortlist that fits your score, budget and city.
