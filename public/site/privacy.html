Privacy policy

This page explains what we collect when you use gurumantra.info, why we collect it, and the choices you have. Last updated: 31 October 2026.

What we collect

When you fill in a form on this website, we collect the details you type in: your name, phone or WhatsApp number, email address, city, entrance exam, percentile, the programme you're interested in, and any question you write. We don't ask for documents, payment details or passwords on this website.

The CAT college predictor and the college finder work in your browser. The percentile you slide or type there is not sent to us unless you also submit a form.

How we use it

We use your details only to provide counselling: to call or message you, to understand your profile, and to send you a shortlist of MBA and PGDM colleges. We may follow up about admissions you asked us to help with.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your details. We share them with a college only when you ask us to help you apply there, or when you agree to it during counselling. Your enquiries are stored securely with our service providers that host this website and its database.

WhatsApp

If you choose to message us on WhatsApp, that conversation is handled by WhatsApp under its own terms and privacy policy.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to stop contacting you, or to correct or delete the details you shared. Message us on WhatsApp at +91 91258 00871 or email gurumantra871@gmail.com, and we'll act on it.

College information

College fees, placements, cutoffs and other figures on this website come from data published by colleges for the latest admission cycle. They can change, so please confirm them with the college before you apply.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us on WhatsApp or by email at gurumantra871@gmail.com.
